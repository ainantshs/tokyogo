Before you go

Get a transit card

Add a Suica or Pasmo card to your phone wallet for trains and shops.

Carry some cash

Many small restaurants and shrines still accept cash only.

Start early

Popular sights are far quieter before 9 am.

Pack light

Stations have many stairs, and hotel rooms are compact.
